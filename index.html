about our brand

Our brand cosmetics are made from natural
ingredients and we are promoting them to the
masses. Shampoo, hair conditioner, soap -
in recycled paper. Creams, oils, washing products
in glass containers with recycled paper labels.
No synthetics and microplastics in cosmetics.
And the packaging from it can be returned for
recycling or reused.

about the creators

My name is Victoria, I’m a master at candle
making and natural cosmetics. In the past, a
cynologist, was engaged in herbs, made
layouts on the Tarot.

I am also a volunteer to save wild birds in
distress, a consultant to the Phoenix Wildlife
Rehabilitation Center, a member of the Society
for the Conservation of Birds of Ukraine with the
support of Birdlife International.

My husband and I live in the Carpathian region
in the city of Chernivtsi. I dream of my center
for the rehabilitation of wild birds, as it is very
difficult to rescue birds in a rented communal
apartment.
